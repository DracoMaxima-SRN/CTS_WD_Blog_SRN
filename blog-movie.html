My Favourite Movies

Posted January 18th 2026

Fantasy, Fiction & Adventure

I love Fantasy, Fiction and Adventure movies because they’re basically my excuse to time-travel, world-hop, and quest from the comfort of a couch. From watching wizards save the world in Harry Potter, trekking across Middle-earth in The Lord of the Rings, to chasing ancient artifacts with Indiana Jones or surviving death-defying stunts with Lara Croft, these films deliver epic action, legendary worlds, and peak nerd satisfaction. They’re packed with magic, myths, and explosions in just the right proportions. Not forgetting they prove that a good movie marathon can feel just as heroic as going on the adventure yourself.

“Not all those who wander are lost.” — The Lord of the Rings



Favourite Franchise

Harry Potter

From bookish Muggle, to film-binge spellcaster, to open-world Hogwarts Legacy explorer and soon leveling up to the HBO Max series — growing up in the wizarding world has been a full-on, nerd-approved adventure.

The Film Series

Spanning over two decades, the Harry Potter film series brought J.K. Rowling’s magical world to life on the big screen, evolving from whimsical beginnings to epic cinematic storytelling.
